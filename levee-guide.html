The Levee (Risk Regime)

Built between your capital and the flood. Is capital reaching for risk, or running from it? One cross-asset read of the whole market, painted on any chart you're viewing.

Overview

01What it does

The Levee reads the market's overall RISK-ON vs RISK-OFF posture from a basket of cross-asset markets (volatility, bonds, credit, equity leadership, commodities, gold, and the dollar) and distills it into one number and one word.

Each market group becomes a vote from -1 (risk-off) to +1 (risk-on). The seven votes combine into one composite and map to a five-state band. Because the read is market-wide, you get the same backdrop on any ticker: a fast top-of-screen context layer before you analyze price.

🧭

It's context, not a trigger

The Levee tells you the weather: the environment your trade lives in. It is not a buy/sell signal. Read the regime, then take your entries from your chart tools (Wolf Chart, Loup Gate).

The engine

02The seven pillars

Each pillar asks one question of the market. Volatility and Bonds carry the most weight: they are the fastest, most reliable tells of the day's risk mood.

PillarThe question it asks
VolatilityIs fear rising or fading? Rising VIX = risk-off. The lead tell.
BondsIs money choosing stocks or safety? Also watches the yield curve, bond-market stress, and the case where stocks and bonds fall together (see below).
CreditIs risky debt holding up? The credit canary. Credit often cracks before stocks do.
Equity + breadthIndex momentum, small-cap leadership, and whether the rally is broad or carried by a few megacaps.
Copper / GoldGrowth or defense? Copper beating gold = growth optimism.
GoldA haven bid, or gold sold alongside stocks (forced selling)? Speaks mainly in a selloff; quiet in a risk-on tape.
Dollar / FXIs liquidity tightening or easing? A surging dollar = tightening = risk-off.

In the Dashboard layout every pillar carries a plain-English read ("Fear spiking", "Money favors stocks", "Credit cracking", "Megacaps only", "Liquidation selling") so you see why the regime is what it is, not just the number.

When bonds are not a haven

Growth scare

Stocks down · bonds UP

Money flees risk into safety. Treasuries rally. Bonds ARE the haven. Classic risk-off.

Rates shock

Stocks down · bonds DOWN

Yields rising while stocks fall, 2022-style. Bonds are NOT a haven. Hiding in long bonds loses on both legs.

The Bonds pillar tells these two apart, and the second one shows up in its read as "Selling stocks & bonds."

The read

03Five states + conviction

The composite maps to a five-state band. The lean bands are symmetric, so the regime registers a risk-on turn about as fast as a risk-off one.

RISK-ON
Strong, broad risk appetite
Risk-On Lean
Tilting toward risk
Neutral
No clear tilt
Risk-Off Lean
Tilting toward safety
RISK-OFF
Deep, broad defense

A conviction tier (HIGH / MED / LOW) appends to the regime line, reading how unanimously the seven pillars agree. Conviction measures agreement, not certainty. The pillars can agree and still be early. The deepest readings matter most: a strong RISK-OFF is a far more meaningful capital-protection flag than a mild lean.

Timing

04Two modes

Default

Confirmed (stable)

Reads each market's prior completed daily bar over a multi-day window. Stable all session, non-repainting. Best for the pre-market top-down and the daily backdrop.

Intraday

Live (intraday)

Reads the developing daily bar, so the regime can shift intraday and catch a same-day turn. Provisional until the close (it repaints intraday by design) and tagged ·LIVE on the panel.

Date alignment. On 1D and intraday charts, Live reads the feed bar of the same date as the chart bar (developing intraday, completed after the close, holding through the weekend, rolling at the next premarket open). Confirmed reads the prior completed day. On charts above 1D (3D, 1W, 1M), both modes read the chart bar's latest daily bar: Live the current day, Confirmed the latest completed day.

⏱️

Freshness: read the stamp

The Levee's feeds are daily. The panel's "· data [date]" stamp shows the date the slowest feed actually reflects. It stamps the worst case, so if the stamp matches the tape, everything does.

One residual, stated plainly: on historical bars above 1D, the volatility pillar can read one day early because VIX closes after stocks. The realtime bar is correct.

If the panel ever looks frozen against the tape while the stamp is current, reload the indicator before assuming a bug. A long-running instance can hold a stale copy of the sector feeds.

Posture

05The action line

A short plain-English call on every layout, built only from the regime state and the pillar votes. The first words say what the market is doing; after the dash, what to do about it.

Momentum on - trade with trendStrong risk-on, pillars agree, the lead pillars onside.
Improving - A-setups onlyRisk-on lean with the pillars agreeing. Be selective.
No regime edge - stay nimbleNeutral, pillars mixed. The regime isn't helping either side.
Pillars split - stand downA directional state, but the pillars or the leads don't back it.
VIX & bonds turning - cautionVolatility and bonds both leaning risk-off. Upside calls are vetoed.
Gold sold with stocks - cautionA mild forced-selling tell. The red "Forced selling" line is reserved for the strong version.
Slipping - tighten stopsRisk-off lean. Defense on.
Risk-off - protect capitalStrong risk-off.
Forced selling - raise cashStrong forced-selling tell: gold dumped with stocks.

If the lead feeds go missing, the line reads "VIX/bond feed down - no read" instead of guessing.

🛡️

Contradiction-proof by construction

Safety reads are checked before any bullish read, and every upside line needs the lead pillars onside. A bullish message can never appear while VIX and Bonds are both risk-off. One message, always internally consistent.

On the chart

06Layouts & rows

  • Strip: a slim one-row read: regime + conviction + every pillar's signed vote, color-coded, with the action line.
  • Dashboard: a full vertical panel with a plain-English read per pillar, tinted by each vote. The regime line carries day N (how long this state has held) and where the score sits in its band; the ▶ ACT row names the driver (the pillar pushing hardest right now); the rotation row carries the day's spread.
  • Phone: regime, score, the two lead pillars, and the action line, sized for the mobile app.

Index Rotation row

A slim line under the regime: SPY / QQQ / DIA / IWM day change, sorted leader to laggard, with a one-word day character. The order is the read. Small caps leading means risk appetite; the Dow leading means defense. Display-only: it never votes in the regime. The regime is the tide; the rotation is today's wave.

Day characterWhat it means
BROAD BID · GROWTH BID · STEADY BIDA green tape, led by small caps, tech, or large caps.
GROWTH LED · DEFENSIVE · BETA BIDA mixed tape: narrow tech leadership, the Dow over beta, or beta bid on a mixed day.
BETA FADINGSmall caps sold first while large caps hold. The classic early de-risk order.
TECH SOLDMoney leaving growth.
SPLIT TAPEA mixed day with a violent gap between leader and laggard. Pick a side.
FLAT TAPENobody moved. Don't force a dead tape.
RED TAPE · RISK PUKEEverything red. PUKE = small caps worst, the liquidation order.
BLOODBATH · MELT-UPThe rare deep days, in gold. All four sold hard or bought hard. Their rarity is the point.
MELT-UP · unconfirmedA melt-up day where the day's internals don't back the move. Read it as "don't chase on the tape's word alone." It forecasts nothing, is never a short signal, and deliberately has no alert.

Sector Rotate print STRIP · DEFAULT OFF

Where is the money going today? One slim line under the rotation row:

IN XLE XLU XLRE▲ · OUT XLV▼ XLF▼ XLY ▶ DEFENSE BID - CAUTION
  • IN = the three SPDR sectors beating SPY hardest, strongest first. OUT = the three lagging SPY most. Both are relative strength vs SPY, so a sector can be green and still be OUT on a stronger tape.
  • The short horizon follows your Regime mode (today in Live, the confirmed window in Confirmed) and is compared against a longer trend window (default 21 days, adjustable).
  • ▲ on an IN sector = its rank is rising against the trend window (new money arriving). ▼ on an OUT sector = its rank is falling (the exit is accelerating). Unflagged = established.
  • The gold read votes across the IN list: DEFENSE BID - CAUTION · GROWTH BID - FOLLOW MONEY · ENERGY BID - FOLLOW MONEY · CYCLICAL BID - FOLLOW MONEY · NOTHING BEATS SPY - STAY SHARP.
  • If too few sector feeds are live it prints SECTOR FEEDS DOWN, never a half-empty list. Display-only: sectors never vote in the regime.

Automation

08Alerts & the daily log

  • Regime change · Flip to Risk-On / Risk-Off · Entered RISK-ON / RISK-OFF: with the optional high-conviction-only filter.
  • Liquidation detected (gold sold with stocks) and VIX spike: always fire, never filtered.
  • Rotation alerts: RISK PUKE, BETA FADING, tape flipped GREEN / RED, BLOODBATH, MELT-UP, SPLIT TAPE. They fire once when the tell appears, not every bar it persists.
  • Sector RISK-OFF x11 (XLK, XLF, XLE, XLV, XLI, XLY, XLP, XLU, XLB, XLRE, XLC): a sector fires when it's red on the day AND lagging SPY by your lag gap. That's money rotating out of the sector, not just a red tape. Once per sector per day, follows your Regime mode, and draws nothing. Create alerts for just the sectors you watch.
  • Guidance changed: fires whenever the action line changes, in any direction. One alert covers the whole read.
  • Daily Regime Log: once per new daily bar, a CSV line with the date, regime, score, conviction, the seven pillar votes, the rotation character, average and spread, and all four index day changes (18 columns). Set an alert on "Any alert() function call" and your alert log becomes a macro journal.
Playbook

09How to read it right

  1. Read the regime first. RISK-ON favors trading with momentum; RISK-OFF favors defense first; Neutral means mixed.
  2. Check conviction. HIGH means the pillars agree. LOW means the tape is arguing with itself.
  3. In Dashboard, scan the pillar reads to see why. Watch for divergence: volatility and gold turning red while the index is still green is an early warning.
  4. Read the rotation row for today's character: the risk-appetite order or the defensive order?
  5. Use Live intraday, Confirmed for the backdrop. Confirmed is your pre-market read; Live tells you if the day is turning.
  6. It's the weather, not the trade. Let the action line set your posture. Take entries from your chart: The Levee sets the backdrop your setups live in.
The honest close

10What it is, and what it isn't

Most indicators imply everything they show is a forecast. The Levee doesn't.

🐊

Deep RISK-OFF is a danger read, not a direction call

The one reading that speaks to what comes next is the deep RISK-OFF band. Treat it as a reason to get smaller and protect: outcomes get wider in both directions. Respect it with position size and protection, not with an automatic short.

Everything else is a measured description of the present. A RISK-ON read means the water is warm today, not that tomorrow is green. RISK-ON is permission and context, never a buy signal, and the action line is worded accordingly.

The two modes, by their jobs: Live answers "what is the tape doing right now." Confirmed answers "what was the completed regime as of yesterday." Neither is a crystal ball. They are the same honest narrator at two shutter speeds.

The division of labor stays clean: The Levee tells you how dangerous the water is. Loup Gate tells you which way price has committed. Your trigger gets you in.
