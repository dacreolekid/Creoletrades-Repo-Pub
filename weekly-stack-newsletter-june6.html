It was a quiet, bullish week — until it wasn't. Monday through Thursday, the indices grinded higher with the Dow pressing all-time highs at 51,760 and tech leading. Then Friday's Non-Farm Payrolls came in hot, yields ripped front-end first, and the entire tape flipped risk-off in a single session.

Macro Breakdown

Why Friday Hit Different

The NFP print came in hot — strong enough to reprice rate-cut expectations instantly. Front-end yields led the charge: the 2-year ripped +2.47% while the 30-year barely moved (+0.42%). That’s a bear flattener — the curve got crushed, with the 2s10s spread dropping -10% to 0.387.

Translation: the market went from "cuts are coming" to "cuts are off the table" in one print. Good news became bad news.

⚠️ THE REGIME: DURATION REPRICING

Stocks down AND bonds down together. That means bonds are NOT the haven. This is yields-driven selling, not a growth scare. Buying TLT here loses both legs — that’s the trap.

The Liquidation Tell

Gold dropped -3.65% on the day. When gold gets sold during a selloff, that’s not fear — that’s forced de-grossing. Money scrambling for cash, selling everything including havens. SOXL lost -30.5%, TNA -10.6%. The leveraged longs got liquidated.

VIX surged to 21.50, kissing the VIX3M term structure at 21.82. When spot vol catches the term structure like that, it’s real fear — not just a coil.

Smart Money Flow

Where the Money Went

🔴 OUT OF Tech, semis (QQQ, SMH, SOXL), small caps (IWM/RTY), long bonds (TLT), and gold — everything got sold
🟢 INTO Cash, the dollar (DXY +0.64% over 100), defensive low-beta (XLV, XLP), value, and relative-strength Dow (DIA)
🟡 HIDING Cash + dollar. When gold is being sold with everything else, the only safe spot is the sideline.

🐋 The Rotation Play

DIA/YM held up best (-1.42% vs NQ -4.79%) and is sitting right on the $508–$509 demand zone with higher-timeframe structure still intact. If the tape stabilizes next week, the Dow is where the bounce starts. But demand the confirmation — don’t catch the knife.
