Straight Talk

What it is, and what it isn't

The Levee tells you how dangerous the water is. Your chart and your trigger still get you in.

It is

  • ⚜ A read of the market environment you're trading in, right now
  • ⚜ A posture guide: when to press, when to get selective, when to protect
  • ⚜ A capital-protection flag when the deep risk-off state prints
  • ⚜ The same read on every ticker and every chart

It isn't

  • ⚜ A buy or sell signal. RISK-ON means warm water today, not a green tomorrow
  • ⚜ A forecast of direction. Deep risk-off means size down, not short everything
  • ⚜ A replacement for structure, entries and your own risk plan
